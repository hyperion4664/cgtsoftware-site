1. Overview

Senua uses artificial intelligence to support selected food-journaling and weekly-insight features. AI processing is used to make user-submitted information easier to understand and organize.

AI is not used to make medical decisions, diagnose health conditions, provide treatment, determine insurance or employment eligibility, or create advertising profiles.

2. AI Features in Senua

Senua may use AI for the following functions:

  • Analyzing a meal photograph submitted by the user;
  • Producing structured meal information and approximate food estimates;
  • Turning pre-computed weekly statistics into a short, readable pattern story;
  • Rewriting approved experiment titles or descriptions for clarity without changing their meaning.

Some Senua features work without AI, including manual meal entry and other manually recorded wellness information.

3. Meal Photo Analysis

When you choose to analyze a meal photograph, Senua may send the image and limited meal context to an AI service provider.

The AI response may include:

  • An estimated meal name;
  • An estimated meal type;
  • An estimated portion description;
  • An approximate calorie range;
  • A confidence value;
  • Structured food-related information used by Senua.
Meal names, ingredients, portions, and calorie ranges generated from photographs are estimates and may be inaccurate.

4. Weekly Pattern Stories

Senua's backend may calculate and validate weekly statistics from your records before AI processing begins.

The AI receives only the prepared statistics and instructions required to turn them into readable text. It is instructed not to invent, infer, round, combine, modify, or independently calculate numbers, dates, durations, thresholds, sample sizes, or differences.

Pattern claims are intended to use only eligible, pre-validated comparisons. Summary values alone are not intended to be used to claim that two behaviors are related.

Step and movement information may appear only as passive background where Senua has already validated an eligible association. Movement is not presented as a fitness score, competition, step target, or exercise program.

5. Information That May Be Sent for AI Processing

For meal-photo analysis The meal photograph you choose to submit, together with limited meal context required for the requested analysis.
For weekly pattern stories Pre-computed and validated statistics, eligible comparison objects, approved experiment information, and instructions required to produce the story.
Technical processing information Limited request metadata necessary to transmit, secure, monitor, and troubleshoot the AI request.

Senua seeks to limit AI requests to the information reasonably necessary for the requested feature.

6. Information Not Intentionally Sent to AI Providers

Senua is designed not to intentionally include the following in normal AI requests:

  • Your name or surname;
  • Your email address;
  • Your password or authentication credentials;
  • Your full Clerk account identifier;
  • Your payment-card or bank-account details;
  • Your complete subscription or billing history;
  • Your contacts, microphone recordings, or precise location;
  • Health information unrelated to the requested AI feature.

You should avoid placing unnecessary personal, confidential, or third-party sensitive information inside meal photographs or free-text notes.

7. AI Service Providers

Senua may use OpenAI's API services to process meal images and validated weekly statistics.

Information submitted to an AI provider is also subject to that provider's applicable privacy, security, and data-processing terms.

Senua may change or add AI service providers where reasonably necessary to maintain, secure, or improve the service. Material changes will be reflected in this page or the Privacy Policy.

8. Model Training and Provider Retention

Senua does not intend to use your meal photographs, journal data, or weekly statistics to train Senua's own public AI model.

Senua uses API-based AI services rather than a consumer chatbot account. Under the provider settings and terms applicable to the service, API inputs and outputs are not intended to be used for model training unless the API customer expressly enables such use.

AI providers may temporarily retain limited request data for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or technical operations in accordance with their applicable policies and service configuration.

Senua will not knowingly opt in to voluntary model-training data sharing for user meal photographs or weekly wellness data without updating this notice and obtaining any consent required by law.

9. Your Choices and Control

  • Meal-photo AI analysis is initiated only when you choose the relevant feature.
  • You may enter meal information manually instead of using photo analysis.
  • You are not required to accept an AI-generated result as correct.
  • You may review, edit, replace, or delete supported journal entries and results.
  • You may stop using AI features while continuing to use supported non-AI features.
  • You may request deletion of your Senua account and associated data.

10. Accuracy and Limitations

AI systems are probabilistic. Their outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, inconsistent, delayed, or unavailable.

Senua does not guarantee the accuracy of AI-generated meal names, ingredients, portion descriptions, calorie ranges, confidence values, weekly narratives, or suggested wording.

Users should review AI-generated content and correct records where appropriate. Senua should not be used where an exact nutritional, clinical, legal, or safety determination is required.

11. Health and Medical Limitations

Senua is a general wellness and self-observation application. AI-generated content is not medical, nutritional, psychological, or treatment advice.

  • Senua does not diagnose dehydration or any health condition;
  • Senua does not diagnose sleep, eating, metabolic, or nutritional disorders;
  • A weekly association does not prove that one behavior caused another;
  • Hydration patterns do not imply that drinking more water would improve sleep, mood, appetite, or health;
  • Movement patterns do not constitute an exercise prescription or fitness recommendation.
Do not use Senua's AI output for emergency, diagnosis, treatment, medication, or other clinical decisions. Seek advice from a qualified professional when needed.

12. Deletion and Retention

When you delete a supported meal entry, Senua is designed to remove the related application-stored meal data and associated AI result from active systems, subject to temporary backup and technical-processing cycles.

When your account is deleted, Senua will delete associated meal photographs, AI results, weekly stories, and related stored data as described in the Privacy Policy and Delete Account page.

Limited technical, security, fraud-prevention, backup, or legally required records may remain for up to 30 days. Third-party providers may apply their own limited retention periods for security or legal purposes.

13. Changes to This Notice

We may update this page when Senua's AI features, providers, processing methods, or legal obligations change. The revised page will display a new “Last updated” date.

Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or request consent before making a material change to how personal or health-related data is used.
