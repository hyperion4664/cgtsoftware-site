3. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the features you use, the information you submit, and the permissions you grant.

3.1 Account and profile information

  • Name and surname, when provided;
  • Email address;
  • Authentication provider and internal user identifiers;
  • Account status, application preferences, and settings.

3.2 Meal and food-journal information

  • Meal photographs you choose to upload;
  • Meal names, types, dates, times, portions, and notes;
  • Food-related observations and feelings that you choose to record;
  • AI-generated meal descriptions and estimated calorie ranges;
  • Weekly summaries and pattern insights generated from your records.

3.3 Hydration information

  • Water-intake entries and timestamps;
  • Daily or weekly hydration totals calculated by the application;
  • Hydration-related preferences you choose within Senua.

3.4 Sleep and movement information

  • Sleep duration and related sleep records you authorize or enter;
  • Daily step counts you authorize Senua to read;
  • Derived daily and weekly values required to provide Senua's insights.

3.5 Subscription and purchase information

  • Product identifier and subscription plan;
  • Subscription status, renewal status, and expiration date;
  • App Store, Google Play, or test-store transaction references.

Senua does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details.

3.6 Technical and usage information

  • Device type, operating system, application version, and language;
  • Technical logs, request timestamps, and error information;
  • Feature usage required to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

Senua does not intentionally access your precise location, contacts, microphone, or advertising identifier as part of its core functionality.

4. Health and Wellness Data

With your explicit permission, Senua may read step-count and sleep information from:

  • Apple Health on iOS; and
  • Health Connect on Android.

You can deny or revoke these permissions through your device settings. Revoking permission stops future access but does not automatically delete information already saved in your Senua account. You may delete your account or contact us to request deletion.

Senua processes the authorized values needed to provide weekly overviews and pattern stories. Movement is used only as passive context and is not used to create a fitness score, competition, calorie-burn target, or exercise program.

Health and wellness information is not sold, used for targeted advertising, shared with data brokers, or used for insurance, lending, employment, or eligibility decisions.

5. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your Senua account;
  • Provide meal, hydration, sleep, movement, and weekly-insight features;
  • Analyze meal photographs that you choose to submit;
  • Generate non-medical summaries of patterns in your own records;
  • Manage subscriptions, entitlements, free access, and usage limits;
  • Synchronize your information across supported devices;
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and investigate technical problems;
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your health or wellness information for third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or advertising profiles.

6. Artificial Intelligence Processing

6.1 Meal-photo analysis

When you choose AI meal analysis, the meal photograph and relevant meal context may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing. The analysis may return a meal name, meal type, portion description, estimated calorie range, confidence value, and other structured food information.

6.2 Weekly pattern stories

Senua may send pre-computed and validated statistics to OpenAI to generate a readable weekly story. The AI is instructed not to calculate new statistics, invent values, provide diagnoses, or claim that one behavior caused another.

6.3 Data minimization

We seek to avoid sending your name, email address, payment information, or other direct account identifiers to the AI model. The information sent is limited to what is reasonably required for the requested feature.

AI output may be incomplete or inaccurate and must not be treated as medical, nutritional, diagnostic, or treatment advice.

11. Data Retention

We retain account and journal information while your account remains active or as reasonably necessary to provide Senua.

When you delete an individual meal or record, Senua is designed to remove the associated record and any linked application-stored image from active systems, subject to temporary backup and technical-processing cycles.

After an account-deletion request is completed, associated personal data is removed from active systems. Limited technical, fraud-prevention, security, backup, or legally required records may be retained for up to 30 days, unless a longer period is required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

12. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion through Senua's in-app account settings or by emailing coskunntirass@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

An account-deletion request is intended to delete:

  • Your Senua account and profile data;
  • Meal records, uploaded meal photographs, notes, and AI results;
  • Hydration, sleep, movement, and feeling records stored by Senua;
  • Weekly stories and related derived information;
  • Senua's internal subscription and entitlement records, except records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Deleting Senua does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play. Subscription cancellation must be completed through the relevant store account settings.
