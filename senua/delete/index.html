Delete Your Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Senua account and the personal data associated with it.

Last updated: July 20, 2026 Data controller: Coşkun Tıraş

How to Delete Your Senua Account

You can request account deletion using either of the following methods.

Method 1: Delete your account in the Senua app

  1. Open Senua and sign in to your account.
  2. Open your profile or account settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion notice and confirm your request.

You may be asked to confirm your identity before the request is completed.

Method 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email from the address associated with your Senua account to:

Request Account Deletion

Use the subject line Senua Account Deletion Request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or identity documents unless we specifically request information needed to verify account ownership.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After your request is verified and processed, deletion includes:

  • Your Senua account and profile information;
  • Meal records, meal photographs, notes, and food-journal entries;
  • AI-generated meal analyses and estimated calorie ranges;
  • Hydration records;
  • Sleep and step information stored by Senua;
  • Feelings, observations, preferences, and application settings;
  • Weekly summaries, pattern stories, and related derived information;
  • Senua's internal subscription, entitlement, and usage records, except where retention is legally or technically required.

Deletion Timeframe and Limited Retention

We will process a valid deletion request as soon as reasonably possible. Personal data will be removed from active Senua systems following completion of the request.

Limited technical, security, fraud-prevention, backup, or legally required records may remain for up to 30 days. A longer period will apply only where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Data retained temporarily during this period will not be used to provide normal Senua features or for advertising.

Subscriptions Are Not Cancelled Automatically

Deleting your Senua account or uninstalling the application does not automatically cancel an active subscription purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play.

To prevent future charges, cancel your subscription through the subscription settings of the store account used to make the purchase.

Apple Health and Health Connect

Deleting your Senua account removes health and wellness values stored in Senua, subject to the limited retention period described above.

Account deletion does not delete original information held by Apple Health or Health Connect. You can manage those permissions and source records separately through your device settings and the relevant health-data service.

What Happens After Deletion

  • You will lose access to your Senua records and weekly stories.
  • Deleted information cannot normally be restored.
  • You may create a new account later, but previous deleted data will not automatically return.
  • An active marketplace subscription may continue until you cancel it separately.
