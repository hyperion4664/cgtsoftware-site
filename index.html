A hiker is lost in the mountains with a phone that is running out. You get a few answers before the line goes quiet. After that it is you, a rescue team on the radio, and a map built from real elevation data.

Read the terrain, steer the team along the tracks they find, and keep the caller's panic and the team's fatigue from tipping over before night does.
