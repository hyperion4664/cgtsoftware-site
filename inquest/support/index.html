Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Inquest has no accounts and does not upload your progress. Restoring from a device backup or moving to a new phone with the platform's transfer tools usually brings it along. Deleting the app removes it.

I found a mistake in a case.

Tell us which case, which paper or page, and what looks wrong. The cases are works of fiction: the people, places, institutions and club rules are invented, and they are not official records. We fix factual slips and unclear evidence in updates.

Which languages does the game support?

The current build is written in Turkish first. An English version will follow, and until then English falls back to the Turkish text.

What data does the app collect?

No account, no ads, no analytics. Read the full Privacy Policy.
