InquestPort Ellery, 1888

You work for the coroner of a harbour town. Records come in, you read them, and you write down only what they support.

In development

What it is

Inquest is a quiet detective game made of paper: ledgers, registers, doctors' notes, receipts and telegrams. There are no chases and no confessions to extract. Each case is a file on your desk, and the work is careful reading.

Ten cases are written so far, all set in the same harbour town. They are works of fiction. People, places, institutions and club rules are invented.

Visit

You have one evening and a morning. Every visit takes an hour and brings new papers to the desk, so you cannot see everything and have to choose.

Link

Put two papers side by side and a fact appears, with its limits written beside it. A missing record is never treated as a "no".

Report

At ten o'clock you write the report. Say what the records show, name what they do not, and leave the verdict to the court. Overreach is stopped before you can send it.

On the desk

Screens are from the current build, which is playable in Turkish. An English version will follow. Inquest is separate from the Signal Station series.
