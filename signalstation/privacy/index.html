Signal Station

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

The short version.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We never receive it.
  • If you buy something, Apple takes the payment. We use RevenueCat to confirm the purchase.
  • If you email us, we get your address and what you write.

Who we are

Signal Station is made by CGT Software, a one-person studio run by Coşkun Tıraş in Turkey. In this policy, "we" means CGT Software. You can reach us at cgtsoftware.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Signal Station saves your progress, such as which case files you have opened and solved, and your settings, in the app's storage on your device. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app does not need an account.

Deleting the app removes this data. If you back up your device (for example to iCloud or a computer), the backup may include the app's data, depending on your device settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your name, card details or payment method.

To unlock purchased content and to restore purchases, we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives your purchase information from Apple and an anonymous identifier created by the app. This identifier is not linked to your name or email address. RevenueCat processes this information for us under its own privacy policy.

Apple also gives us sales reports, which are aggregated and do not identify individual players.

When you contact us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message. The pre-filled message in the app also includes the app version and your device's operating system version, which you can read and delete before sending. It does not include game progress or any personal identifier.

We use your message only to reply and to fix the problem you describe. We keep it as long as we need it for that purpose and for our records, and we delete it if you ask.

What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

Signal Station is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has written to us, contact us and we will delete the message.

Your rights

The app holds no personal data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to export or delete there. For emails you have sent us, you can ask us to show, correct or delete them. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK give you these rights. Data held by Apple or RevenueCat is covered by their policies, and you can ask them directly.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle data differently, for example by adding a new service, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches players.

Contact

Questions about this policy: cgtsoftware.support@gmail.com
