Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and use the restore option in the Purchases section. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. If it does not work, email us and tell us which Apple ID you used (not your password).

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Signal Station has no accounts and does not upload your progress. Restoring from a device backup or moving to a new iPhone with Apple's transfer tools usually brings it along. Deleting the app removes it.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings, go to the Progress section and choose reset. You confirm with a second tap. This cannot be undone.

I want a refund.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I found a mistake in a case file.

Tell us which file and which card or page, and what looks wrong. The case files are works of fiction inspired by real accidents, and they are not official accident reports. We fix factual slips and unclear evidence in updates.

What data does the app collect?

No account, no ads, no analytics. Read the full Privacy Policy.
